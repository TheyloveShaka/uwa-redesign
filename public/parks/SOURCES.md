Content notes on the 10 wildlife-reserve images (added in this pass, verified by opening each file): these are the `og:image` UWA's own page markup names as the featured photo for each reserve page. Two are worth flagging: `matheniko-wildlife-reserve-camels.webp` shows a herd of domestic camels — not any species in Matheniko's own Fauna list (lion, leopard, cheetah, giraffe, eland, roan antelope, Bright's gazelle) and not native Ugandan wildlife; `kabwoya-wildlife-reserve-elephants.webp` (source filename `elephtans.jpg`, UWA's own typo) shows an elephant family, and elephants are likewise absent from Kabwoya's own stated Fauna list. Both are used as-is because they are genuinely the images UWA's own page markup designates, not because they depict the reserve's named wildlife — see the `todo` array on each entry in `src/data/reserves.ts`. Toro Semliki Wildlife Reserve has no image row here: its page's `og:image` resolves to the generic `UWA-Rangers.jpeg` placeholder already catalogued above, not a reserve-specific photo, so nothing new was downloaded for it and its `image` field in `reserves.ts` is `null`.
